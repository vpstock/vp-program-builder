Job and settings

Blue cells are inputs. Hours come straight from the guideline (Estimating Guideline, approved 19 Oct 2026, proposed hours where one exists). Change quantities, never the hours. A custom line is flagged and needs a reason.

Areas and quantities

An area is a level, floor or zone. Each area gets a Victorian Projects task and a DJB Electrical task. Job-level lines (site setup, job balance set-up) and all commissioning lines are programmed once, at the start and end.

Program

Days = hours x difficulty / (people x hours per person per day), rounded up, minimum 1. DJB for an area starts the day after our crew finishes it and runs alongside our crew on the next area. Commissioning is last because ceilings close once sheeted.
